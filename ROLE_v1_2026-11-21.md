# [SUPERSEDED 2026-11-24 by ROLE.md v2] Role v1: Head of Robot Software, AI & Simulation

**Company:** Ubanthu Robotics — Norm 100, autonomous restroom-cleaning mobile manipulator (FANUC CRX-5iA 6-axis arm on a 2WD base; Jetson Orin NX; Orbbec depth cameras; 2D LiDAR; steam/water/chemistry cleaning; contamination detection + verification). India-first (Bengaluru, IISc incubation).

**Brief (founders, 2026-11-21):** a person who can build the company's **software stack**: the AI models, the software that **interacts with the hardware stack**, and the **simulation pipeline (real → sim → real)**. Has worked on **6-axis robot arms**, **navigation**, has **hardware knowledge**, can **build ML models from scratch**, and has **extensive ML knowledge**. Does **not** need to be deeply involved in building the robot hardware itself.

**Talent bar:** top-tier builders — IISc, IITs, BITS, IIITs, or strong foreign universities / companies, if willing to move to India (Bengaluru).

## Must-haves

1. **Robot software architecture:** ROS 2 (or equivalent), drivers, real-time control interfaces, state machines/behaviour trees, deployment on edge compute (Jetson), logging, testing/CI.
2. **Manipulation on a real 6-axis arm:** motion planning (MoveIt 2 or similar), force/compliance, tool changes, hand-eye calibration.
3. **Mobile navigation:** SLAM / localisation / Nav2-type planning on a real base.
4. **ML from scratch:** designs, trains and ships models (perception: detection/segmentation; ideally learning-based control), not just fine-tunes APIs. Strong fundamentals.
5. **Simulation & sim-to-real:** Isaac Sim / Isaac Lab / MuJoCo / Gazebo; synthetic data; domain randomisation; closing the real→sim→real loop.
6. **Hardware literacy:** sensors (depth cameras, LiDAR, F/T), embedded interfaces (CAN, EtherCAT, serial), can debug with hardware engineers.

## Nice-to-haves

- Shipped robots to real customers / field deployments (uptime, recovery, remote ops).
- Learning-based manipulation (imitation learning, diffusion policy, VLA, RL).
- Perception on wet/reflective surfaces; anomaly/defect detection (≈ contamination detection).
- Team lead / hiring experience; startup experience.
- Publications at RSS/CoRL/ICRA/IROS/CVPR or notable open-source maintainership.
